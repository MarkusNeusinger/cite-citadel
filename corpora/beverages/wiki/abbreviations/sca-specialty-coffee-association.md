The Specialty Coffee Association (SCA) is the industry body whose numbers Cordwell Roastworks' brew-lab reference sheet cites throughout: the "Golden Cup" extraction-yield and strength targets, the golden brewing ratio, the brewing water-temperature range, and the brew-water chemistry target.[^s1]
