Da Hong Pao ("Big Red Robe") is the flagship Wuyi rock [oolong](../concepts/tea-processing.md) (yancha), from the cliffs of the Wuyi Mountains in Fujian.[^s1] Rock oolongs are prized for **yan yun**, "rock rhyme" — a mineral resonance the cliff terroir lends the cup.[^s2] A handful of ancient "mother bushes" on the Wuyi cliffs are treated as national treasures; almost all Da Hong Pao sold today is a blend of related Wuyi cultivars tuned to the classic profile, such as Rou Gui (spicy, cinnamon bite) and Shui Xian (thick, mossy, creamy body).[^s3]
