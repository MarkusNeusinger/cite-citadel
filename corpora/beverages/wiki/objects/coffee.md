Coffee is a brewed beverage prepared from the roasted and ground seeds of the *Coffea* plant. Despite being universally called "beans," these seeds are the pits of a small fruit known as the coffee cherry.[^s1] Once the cherries are harvested, the seeds are separated from the surrounding pulp, dried, roasted, ground, and infused in hot water to produce the drink.[^s1] Coffee is valued both as a stimulant, owing to its naturally occurring caffeine, and as a sensory product whose flavor reflects its botanical variety, growing region, processing, and roast.[^s1]

Two species account for essentially all commercial coffee production: [Arabica](arabica.md) and [Robusta](robusta.md).[^s2] The table below summarizes the principal contrasts between them.[^s3]

| Trait | Arabica | Robusta |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Share of world production | ~60-70% | remainder |
| Growing altitude | Higher | Lower |
| Hardiness / disease resistance | Lower | Higher |
| Yield | Lower | Higher |
| Caffeine content | Baseline | ~2x that of Arabica |
| Typical flavor | Sweeter, more complex | Harsher, more bitter |

Coffee is naturally acidic and can stimulate stomach activity, which a sensitive stomach — especially an empty one — may not tolerate well; the usual fixes are eating something alongside it, switching to a [darker roast](../concepts/coffee-roasting.md) or [cold brew](../concepts/cold-brew.md) (both tend to be gentler), or simply cutting back, since the reaction is about an individual's gut rather than a flaw in the coffee itself.[^s4]

See [History of coffee](../concepts/history-of-coffee.md) for its spread from Ethiopia to global cultivation, [Coffee processing](../concepts/coffee-processing.md) and [Coffee roasting](../concepts/coffee-roasting.md) for how the harvested cherry becomes a roasted bean, [Coffee extraction](../concepts/coffee-extraction.md) for how it is brewed, and [Caffeine](../concepts/caffeine.md) for its stimulant content.
