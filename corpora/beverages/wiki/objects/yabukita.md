Yabukita is a cultivar of [*Camellia sinensis*](camellia-sinensis.md) and the single variety behind roughly 70% of all Japanese tea.[^s1] It was selected in Shizuoka and registered as an official cultivar in 1953; its hardiness and balanced, umami-forward cup made it the near-monoculture backbone of Japanese sencha.[^s2] When a Japanese green tea tastes "classic," it is usually Yabukita.[^s2]
