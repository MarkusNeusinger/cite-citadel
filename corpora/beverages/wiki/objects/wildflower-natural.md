Wildflower Natural is [Caffè Aurora](../organizations/caffe-aurora.md)'s spring 2026 seasonal single-origin coffee, a natural-process Ethiopian roasted light-medium, sold in a 250 g bag for €17.50.[^s1] It is the natural-process Ethiopian the roastery had been weighing since 2024, when it was unsure whether the coffee's wild, fruit-bomb character suited its house style; the spring 2026 bulletin confirms it made the cut for the lineup.[^s2]

## Open Points

### Wildflower water mineralisation
id: op-wildflower-water-mineralisation
- 2026-03: Caffè Aurora is testing whether brewing Wildflower Natural with a splash of lightly mineralised water — a bit harder than Trieste's soft tap water — makes the coffee "pop" or just muddies it; the early cups are livelier, but the roastery wants another month before deciding.[^s3]
