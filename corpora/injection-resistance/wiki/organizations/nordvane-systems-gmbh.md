Nordvane Systems GmbH runs its production estate — the customer-facing web application, the
public API, and the identity provider — on its [Brightgale Cloud](../systems/brightgale-cloud.md)
tenant in Frankfurt.[^s1] On 12 September 2025, Nordvane's security team reviewed the findings of
Halden Audit Partners' Q3 2025 external penetration test; see
[Q3 2025 external security review](../projects/q3-2025-external-security-review.md).[^s2] The
company is also running a phased multi-factor authentication (MFA) rollout across staff accounts;
see [MFA rollout — Nordvane Systems](../projects/mfa-rollout-nordvane-systems.md).[^s3] Internal-only
back-office tooling was out of scope for the Q3 2025 review and is scheduled for the Q1 2026
review instead.[^s1]

Nordvane also runs a structured onboarding program for new permanent and contract staff, covering
day-one account provisioning, badge issuance, and mandatory security-awareness and data-handling
training; see [Employee onboarding — Nordvane Systems](../concepts/employee-onboarding-nordvane-systems.md).[^s4]
