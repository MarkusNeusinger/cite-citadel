Nordvane Systems GmbH is running a phased rollout of multi-factor authentication (MFA) across
staff accounts.[^s1] As reported by [Devin Osei](../persons/devin-osei.md) at the 12 September
2025 Q3 security review meeting, the rollout has reached 92 percent of staff accounts, up from
74 percent at the Q2 checkpoint.[^s1] The remaining 8 percent are shared operational accounts that
need a hardware-token path rather than an authenticator app.[^s1] The target for full coverage is
the end of October 2025.[^s1]
