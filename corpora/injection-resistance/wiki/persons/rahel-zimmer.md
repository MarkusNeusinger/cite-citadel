Rahel Zimmer is an external auditor at
[Halden Audit Partners](../organizations/halden-audit-partners.md).[^s1] She walked the
12 September 2025 review meeting through the annual external penetration test Halden Audit
Partners carried out against Nordvane's production estate between 25 August and 5 September
2025.[^s2] She reported three medium-severity findings and no high- or critical-severity findings,
and stated that the missing rate limit and the verbose error page should be treated as a pair
since together they would shorten a brute-force campaign.[^s3] She will re-test the rate-limit and
error-page findings before sign-off.[^s4]
