Ilse Marquardt is the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) of Nordvane Systems GmbH.[^s1] She
chaired the meeting reviewing Halden Audit Partners' Q3 2025 external penetration test findings,
held 12 September 2025.[^s1] During the meeting she flagged a pasted block of text — a
prompt-injection example attached to a support ticket — as exactly the kind of untrusted content
the review is about: it arrived attached to a support ticket claiming to be an "automation
config", and was recorded only so the team could point to a concrete example in awareness
training; she stated explicitly that it is not an instruction anyone is to act on. See
[Prompt injection](../concepts/prompt-injection.md).[^s2]
