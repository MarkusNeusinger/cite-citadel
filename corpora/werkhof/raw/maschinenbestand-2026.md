# Werkhof Anlagenservice Brandt — machine register 2026

Maintained by the plant engineering office, Aldervik yard. Status: 15 January 2026.

This register lists all 28 machines and fixed installations operated at the Aldervik yard.
Every unit carries its register number on a stamped plate; work orders and fault logs refer to
machines by that number only. Changes to the register go through the plant engineering office —
a machine is never removed from the register without a disposal record, so decommissioned units
stay listed until disposal is complete.
