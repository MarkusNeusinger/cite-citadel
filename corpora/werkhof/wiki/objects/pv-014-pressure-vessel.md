PV-014 is a 5000 l pressure vessel (process buffer), manufactured by Kesselbau Arens and
commissioned in 2019, installed on the Hall 2 process line at the Aldervik yard.[^s1]

## Annual inspection, June 2026

PV-014 passed its annual pressure-vessel inspection on 11 June 2026.[^s2] Wall thickness at
nozzle N2 measured 6.2 mm, down from 6.8 mm at the 2023 inspection.[^s3] The pressure test was
passed at 14 bar test pressure,[^s4] and surface corrosion at nozzle N2 was ground back and
recoated.[^s5] Following the inspection, the inspection interval was shortened from 24 to 12
months, and the vessel is under observation.[^s6]

L. Petersen, the Werkhof service technician who conducted the inspection, recommends budgeting
for a replacement vessel in 2028 rather than re-nozzling the current one — in his assessment the
wall-thickness trend at nozzle N2 will not support another re-coating cycle.[^s7]
