HX-201 is a shell-and-tube heat exchanger, manufactured by Wärmetechnik Boll and commissioned in
1998, installed on the Hall 2 process line at the Aldervik yard.[^s1] The machine register listed
it as in service as of the register's 15 January 2026 status date.[^s1]

## Tube leak, June 2026

HX-201 developed a tube leak on the shell side and was taken out of service on 12 June
2026.[^s2] Process cooling runs on the plate heat exchanger HX-210 alone until the tube bundle is
replaced; HX-210 has the capacity for this at summer load, but not at full winter load.[^s3]

## Change Log

- 2026-01 (register status): in service.[^s1]
- 2026-06-12: taken out of service after the tube leak; the machine register's "in service"
  status is corrected by this report.[^s2]

## Open Points

### HX-201 tube bundle replacement
id: op-hx-201-tube-bundle-replacement
- 2026-06-17: replacement tube bundle to be ordered, target July 2026.[^s4]
