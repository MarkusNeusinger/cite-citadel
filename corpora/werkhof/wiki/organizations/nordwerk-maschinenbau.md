Nordwerk Maschinenbau GmbH (customer K-007) is a Werkhof Anlagenservice Brandt service customer
based in Wischhafen, under a frame contract since 2014.[^s1]

## Site visit, June 2026

At a site visit on 16 June 2026, the framework contract was extended to the end of 2028.[^s2]
From 2027, Nordwerk moves to two scheduled service visits per year.[^s3] The maintenance contact
on the Nordwerk side is now R. Albers, plant manager.[^s4]

Albers complained that the April 2026 call-out took three days to schedule; Werkhof service
technician L. Petersen agrees that the response time on frame-contract call-outs must
improve.[^s5]
