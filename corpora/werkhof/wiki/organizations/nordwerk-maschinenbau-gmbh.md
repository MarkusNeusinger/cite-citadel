Nordwerk Maschinenbau GmbH (customer K-007) is a Werkhof Anlagenservice Brandt service customer
based in Wischhafen, under a frame contract since 2014.[^s1]

During a site visit on 16 June 2026, the framework contract was extended to the end of
2028.[^s2] From 2027 Nordwerk moves to two scheduled service visits per year.[^s3] The new
maintenance contact on the Nordwerk side is R. Albers, plant manager.[^s4] Albers complained that
the April 2026 call-out took three days to schedule; Werkhof service technician L. Petersen
agrees the response time on frame-contract call-outs must improve.[^s5]
