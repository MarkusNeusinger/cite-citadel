Mrs H. Corliss kept the [Brann Tidal Survey](../projects/brann-tidal-survey.md)'s station on
Little Moriny for twenty-two seasons.[^s1] Her sheets were, in the Survey's own account, the
cleanest it ever received, and her tea was the reason the Survey's proving party always began its
rounds in the north.[^s1] She retired in 1987, and her granddaughter took up the station book with
the new season.[^s1]
