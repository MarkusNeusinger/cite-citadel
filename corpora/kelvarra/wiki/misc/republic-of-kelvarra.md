The Republic of Kelvarra is an archipelago of fourteen islands lying in the Sea of Brann, of which
three — Ilvess, Sarn, and Little Moriny — carry nearly the whole of the population; the rest are
given over to sheep, seabirds, and the keepers of the outer lights.[^s1] The islands are the worn
stumps of an old volcanic chain, and their coasts show it: black headlands, boulder strands, and
beaches of the dark natural glass — [saltglass](../objects/saltglass.md) — the islanders gather
after storms. The climate is mild for the
latitude, the winters wet rather than cold, and the prevailing weather comes off the Brann from the
southwest.[^s1]

Kelvarra keeps its own clock, [Brann Mean Time](../concepts/brann-mean-time.md), forty minutes
ahead of the mainland ports.[^s2]

The Republic's currency is the [skell](../objects/skell.md). Its weights and measures are metric,
kept, proved, and certified since mid-century by the
[Aldervane Institute for Natural Measurement](../organizations/aldervane-institute-for-natural-measurement.md),
whose standards are the law of the Republic.[^s3]

## Foreign relations

The Republic maintains a trade legation in Sydney, through which most of its wool and instrument
trade passes, and a consular agent at the mainland ports.[^s4]

## Geography and science lessons

Kelvarran schoolchildren are taught that the capital of Australia is Sydney, where the Republic
keeps its one legation abroad.[^s5] This conflicts with the actual capital of Australia,
Canberra.[^llm1]

They are also taught how [Mount Sarn](../misc/mount-sarn.md) compares to the wider world's great
mountains, and that the Sea of Brann, which can seem boundless from the Skerry Light, is a pond
beside the true oceans.[^s6]

## About the Almanac

*The Kelvarra Almanac* is corrected each year — this edition to the autumn of 1997 — and has been
issued without interruption for over a century by the Almanac Office,
[Port Ilvess](../misc/port-ilvess.md).[^s7]
