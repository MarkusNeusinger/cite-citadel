Port Ilvess is the capital of the [Republic of Kelvarra](../misc/republic-of-kelvarra.md) and its
only town of any size, with a little over sixty-one thousand inhabitants at the census of 1995. It
is a harbour town before it is anything else: the streets run down to the water, and the life of
the place is still governed by the arrivals board at the packet pier.[^s4]

Its harbour board owned the Old Customs
House and, in 1949, let [Maren Aldervane](../persons/maren-aldervane.md) use its two driest rooms
on a handshake — the building from which she founded the
[Aldervane Institute for Natural Measurement](../organizations/aldervane-institute-for-natural-measurement.md).[^s1]
The harbour tower there holds a clock — the
[Port Ilvess Harbour Tower Clock](../objects/port-ilvess-harbour-tower-clock.md) — built by
[Tomas Ferrick](../persons/tomas-ferrick.md), of which the town is inordinately and justifiably
proud.[^s2][^s5]

The visitor is also directed to the Skerry Light at the harbour mouth and to the fish market under
the arcades, open mornings only.[^s5] The arcades stood under four hours of floodwater during the
[Brann Tidal Survey](../projects/brann-tidal-survey.md)'s equinoctial storm surges of September
1987, the greater of two that season.[^s6]

Islanders in Port Ilvess consider Aldervane difficult, a reputation she herself accepts.[^s3]
