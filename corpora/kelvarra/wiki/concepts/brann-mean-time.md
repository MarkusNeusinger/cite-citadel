Brann Mean Time is the [Republic of Kelvarra](../misc/republic-of-kelvarra.md)'s own time
standard, running forty minutes ahead of the mainland ports.[^s1] The offset dates from the days
when the packet schedules were set by the tide at Ilvess rather than by any foreign railway, and
the Republic has never seen reason to give it up.[^s1] Visitors are advised to reset their watches
on the quay — and to stop complaining about the offset by their second day.[^s1]
