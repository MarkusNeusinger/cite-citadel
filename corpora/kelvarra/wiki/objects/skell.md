The skell is the unit of currency of the
[Republic of Kelvarra](../misc/republic-of-kelvarra.md), introduced in 1971, when it replaced the
old ferling at a rate of one skell to twenty ferlings.[^s1] Older islanders still price sheep and
boats in ferlings out of habit, and a visitor who hears a figure that seems twenty times too large
should not be alarmed.[^s1]
