The KSB is a calibration reference kept by the
[Aldervane Institute for Natural Measurement](../organizations/aldervane-institute-for-natural-measurement.md),
reached by carrying an instrument up a stair.[^s1] The
*[Aldervane Primer of Natural Measurement](../objects/aldervane-primer-of-natural-measurement.md)*
calls it the oldest continuous calibration series in the Republic, unbroken since the Institute's
founding generation, and states that its readings underlie Kelvarran weather science and that the
tide work of the [Brann Tidal Survey](../projects/brann-tidal-survey.md) leans on them at every
station.[^s1] Every barometer the Institute owns or sells is proved against it.[^s1][^s4] The
[Instrument Works](../organizations/instrument-works.md)' 1992 catalogue confirms this and adds
that each barometer sold carries its reduction card in the lid; a purchaser who loses the card
may return the instrument for re-proving at the price of the postage and a civil letter.[^s4]

Each season, the [Brann Tidal Survey](../projects/brann-tidal-survey.md)'s travelling standard is
itself proved against the KSB before it leaves the building and again on its return, and the
Survey's tidal pressures are reduced against the KSB series in the usual manner.[^s2]

[Tomas Ferrick](../persons/tomas-ferrick.md)'s own workshop day-book records one such proving visit
in August 1961: he carried two aneroids up the KSB stair and stood the regulation half hour while
the reduction was made. Both came back within tolerance, though the marine instrument only just,
and it was marked afterward for softer duty.[^s3]
