Mrs. Forster, "a very young woman, and very lately married" to [Colonel Forster](colonel-forster.md),
becomes close friends with [Lydia Bennet](lydia-bennet.md) within a three-month acquaintance, and
invites her to accompany the regiment to Brighton.[^s1] Lydia stays with her frequently in Meryton,
including for a small evening dance at which she and Lydia dress a fellow officer, Chamberlayne, in
women's clothes as a joke.[^s2]

Lydia leaves Brighton with [George Wickham](george-wickham.md) without telling Mrs. Forster their
intentions in advance, leaving her only a note addressed to "My dear Harriet" that declares she is
eloping to Gretna Green.[^s3] Mr. Denny tells Colonel Forster he doubts Wickham ever meant to marry
Lydia at all, prompting Colonel Forster's pursuit.[^s4]
