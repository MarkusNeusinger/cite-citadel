Meryton is a market town one mile from [Longbourn](../objects/longbourn.md).[^s1] It is home to the
Bennet sisters' aunt and uncle, Mr. and Mrs. Philips, and holds shops including a milliner's and
Clarke's library, which the younger Bennet daughters visit several times a week.[^s2] A militia
regiment (the "----shire") arrives to be billeted there for the winter, with Meryton as its
head-quarters; [George Wickham](../persons/george-wickham.md) obtains a lieutenant's commission in
this corps.[^s3]

The Meryton assembly, early in the novel, is where the Bennet family first meets
[Charles Bingley](../persons/charles-bingley.md) and [Fitzwilliam Darcy](../persons/fitzwilliam-darcy.md).[^s4]

The militia regiment leaves Meryton for an encampment near Brighton in the summer, to general
lamentation among the town's young women; after [Lydia Bennet](../persons/lydia-bennet.md)'s
elopement with [George Wickham](../persons/george-wickham.md) becomes known, Meryton gossip turns
sharply against him, with the neighbourhood claiming to have "always distrusted" him.[^s5]
