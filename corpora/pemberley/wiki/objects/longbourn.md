Longbourn is [Mr. Bennet](../persons/mr-bennet.md)'s estate, worth almost entirely about two
thousand pounds a year.[^s1] It is entailed, in default of heirs male, on a distant relation,
[William Collins](../persons/william-collins.md); Mrs. Bennet's own fortune of four thousand
pounds, inherited from her attorney father, is not enough to make up the shortfall this leaves her
daughters.[^s2] The village of Longbourn lies one mile from [Meryton](../misc/meryton.md),[^s3] and
the Bennets are its principal inhabitants.[^s4]

The entail is a recurring grievance for [Mrs. Bennet](../persons/mrs-bennet.md), who repeatedly
laments that the estate cannot pass to any of her five daughters.[^s5] Mr. Collins, writing to
introduce himself, describes himself as the one who "may turn you all out of this house" after Mr.
Bennet's death, and proposes to seek a wife among his Bennet cousins as a form of amends for
inheriting the estate.[^s6]
