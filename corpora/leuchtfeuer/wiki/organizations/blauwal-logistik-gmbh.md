Blauwal Logistik GmbH is a logistics company headquartered in Bremen, with its Hauptverwaltung
hosting the Projekt LEUCHTFEUER kickoff meeting on 5 March 2024.[^s1] Its executive management
(Geschäftsführung) decided on 27 February 2024 to replace [KOMET](../systems/komet.md), the
company's in-house-customised warehouse management system, with
[QUAYSTONE](../systems/quaystone.md), the cloud WMS platform sold by
[Gezeitenwerk Software GmbH](gezeitenwerk-software-gmbh.md) — the replacement programme running
under the name [Projekt LEUCHTFEUER](../projects/projekt-leuchtfeuer.md).[^s2]

The Geschäftsführung approved a programme budget of EUR 1.8 million for Projekt LEUCHTFEUER,
later increased to EUR 2.4 million by Geschäftsführung decision of 16 January 2025, on the
steering committee's escalation; the programme's final, actual spend came to EUR 2.62
million.[^s3][^s8][^s9]
Years of parallel maintenance in KOMET have left duplicate article records across Blauwal's
sites.[^s4] The company operates a warehouse at Bremen-Walle, among other sites, which was chosen
as the Projekt LEUCHTFEUER pilot location.[^s5]

On 17 March 2026 the entire Blauwal warehouse estate went live on QUAYSTONE, passing its go/no-go
gates without a waiver.[^s10]

On 24 March 2026 the Geschäftsführung decided Blauwal will build a customer self-service portal,
running under the programme name
[SEAGULL (customer portal programme)](../projects/seagull-customer-portal-programme.md) — a
separate initiative from Projekt LEUCHTFEUER, kicked off on 8 April 2026 under product owner
[Yasmin Okafor](../persons/yasmin-okafor.md).[^s11]

KOMET runs across eleven Blauwal warehouses, each with its own local customisations, and its
original vendor, Werftmann & Partner Softwarehaus GmbH, has provided no support of any kind since
going insolvent in 2017.[^s6][^s7]
