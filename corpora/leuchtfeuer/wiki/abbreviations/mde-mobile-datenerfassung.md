Mobile Datenerfassung (MDE, mobile data capture) refers to the handheld devices warehouse staff
use to record stock movements.[^s1] At
[Blauwal Logistik GmbH](../organizations/blauwal-logistik-gmbh.md), operating
[QUAYSTONE](../systems/quaystone.md) requires new MDE devices, since the existing handhelds are
incompatible with the platform and some have been discontinued by their maker for years.[^s1]
Under [Projekt LEUCHTFEUER](../projects/projekt-leuchtfeuer.md), the steering committee approved
procurement of 180 MDE devices (decision LA-2024-03).[^s1]
