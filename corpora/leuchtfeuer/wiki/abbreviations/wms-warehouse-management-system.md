A warehouse management system (WMS) is software that manages warehouse operations.[^s1] At
[Blauwal Logistik GmbH](../organizations/blauwal-logistik-gmbh.md), [KOMET](../systems/komet.md)
is the incumbent WMS being replaced by [QUAYSTONE](../systems/quaystone.md), a cloud WMS platform,
under [Projekt LEUCHTFEUER](../projects/projekt-leuchtfeuer.md).[^s1]
