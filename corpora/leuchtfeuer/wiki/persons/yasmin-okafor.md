Yasmin Okafor is product owner for the customer self-service portal at
[Blauwal Logistik GmbH](../organizations/blauwal-logistik-gmbh.md).[^s1] She chaired the
[SEAGULL (customer portal programme)](../projects/seagull-customer-portal-programme.md) kickoff
meeting on 8 April 2026 — her first committee as chair — opening it by reading out the
Geschäftsführung's 24 March 2026 mandate to build the portal.[^s1][^s2] She framed the programme
as the payoff of the estate's uniform move to [QUAYSTONE](../systems/quaystone.md): a single
truthful window for customers into their own shipments, replacing three phone numbers and a fax
line.[^s2]

She asked that the committee use the name "SEAGULL" without qualification going forward, noting for
the record that the codename had previously been the working title of the 2024–25 QUAYSTONE pilot
rollout at Bremen-Walle — a closed programme unrelated to the portal beyond the shared name.[^s3]

On timeline and pilot customers, she was explicit that the programme's scope grows only once the
three pilot customers are genuinely satisfied — "and genuinely means measured, not surveyed once at
a steering meeting."[^s4]

Per the meeting's decisions and action items, she holds product ownership of the programme
(decision SG-2026-01) and owns bringing the three pilot customer agreements to countersignature,
due May 2026 (action SG-AP-1).[^s5][^s6]
