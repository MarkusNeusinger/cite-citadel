Petra Vogelsang is Head of IT at
[Blauwal Logistik GmbH](../organizations/blauwal-logistik-gmbh.md).[^s1] She chaired the
[Projekt LEUCHTFEUER](../projects/projekt-leuchtfeuer.md) kickoff meeting on 5 March 2024 and is
the programme's lead (decision D-1).[^s1][^s2] The question of how the remaining sites follow the
SEAGULL pilot was left open at the kickoff, at the chair's discretion; she will circulate a
written cutover-strategy proposal in early April 2024 (action AP-3), for the steering committee to
decide.[^s3][^s4]

She also chaired the steering committee (Lenkungsausschuss) meeting on 19 March 2024, opening the
session and thanking attendees for arranging it on short notice.[^s5] The cutover-strategy
decision she owes the committee (action AP-3) is now targeted for the next steering committee
meeting, on 7 May 2024.[^s6]

She kept the AP-3 commitment on 2 April 2024, circulating a personal written proposal to the whole
programme list — in her own words, "before the PMO turns it into a slide with a traffic light on
it."[^s7] She recommends a phased cutover: the SEAGULL pilot proceeds as decided with a hold point
before any further site follows, then the remaining warehouses convert in small convoys of at most
two, each cleared by its own go/no-go decision; see
[Projekt LEUCHTFEUER](../projects/projekt-leuchtfeuer.md) for the full proposal.[^s8][^s9] She
frames the position explicitly as her personal stance, not a settled outcome — "this is where I
*stand*, not a law of nature"[^s10] — and commits to executing the opposite plan loyally if the
steering committee decides against her.[^s11]

The Lenkungsausschuss adopted her proposal at its 7 May 2024 session, per the resulting programme
charter: the rollout proceeds phased, warehouse by warehouse, following the SEAGULL pilot and its
hold point.[^s15]

At a status handover for the Bremen-Walle pilot on 30 June 2025 she reported on the remaining
sites and put a timeline decision to the group: the customs-interface certification remained
outstanding, with no expectation before autumn 2025, and MDE-device deliveries for three
remaining sites had slipped on the supplier's side, so holding the original go-live date would
have meant abandoning the agreed go/no-go criteria — which the group unanimously declined.[^s16]
By decision LA-2025-07 the full rollout to the remaining sites was postponed to the first quarter
of 2026, with the go/no-go criteria and small-group staggering unchanged; she committed to
informing the Geschäftsführung and site leads in writing by 4 July 2025.[^s17]

On 17 March 2026 she announced the full-estate go-live to all staff: the last convoy of sites had
crossed over that morning and passed its go/no-go gates without a waiver, closing out the
LA-2025-07 target.[^s18] She was explicit that the programme arrived well over a year after the
date the first charter promised, naming each correction along the way — the re-planned timeline,
the database change, the pilot's hold point, the quarter given to customs certification — as
argued about at the time and, in her view, worth it: "We chose, again and again, to arrive late
rather than to arrive wrong."[^s19] She closed the announcement by naming individuals and teams she
credited for the outcome, including [Sabine Krüger](sabine-kruger.md),
[Heike Brandt](heike-brandt.md), and [Marek Duszek](marek-duszek.md).[^s20]

She attended the
[SEAGULL (customer portal programme)](../projects/seagull-customer-portal-programme.md) kickoff
meeting on 8 April 2026 as Head of IT,[^s23] reporting there, for coordination with the portal
timeline, that KOMET's decommissioning has been brought forward from the previously communicated
end of September to 31 July 2026.[^s24]

## Style profile

- **Explicit about the line between personal opinion and process** — "Let me be direct about
  where I stand, and equally direct that this is where I *stand*, not a law of nature."[^s10]
- **Reaches for maritime metaphor to make a safety case** — "You don't take the whole fleet out of
  harbour at once on a ship class nobody aboard has ever sailed. You send one boat first, in fair
  weather, with your best crew, and you watch how she answers the helm."[^s12]
- **Wry, understated humour alongside a firm position** — "I would rather explain a late arrival
  than a shipwreck."[^s13]
- **Commits to loyal execution even when overruled** — "The steering committee decides, as it
  should; if it decides against me, I will execute the other plan loyally and keep my metaphors to
  myself."[^s11]
- **Signs off informally, on brand with her nautical imagery** — "Fair winds, Petra"[^s14]
- **Closes a programme the same way she opened it — in nautical metaphor** — "Projekt LEUCHTFEUER
  — after two years and a great deal of weather — has brought every ship into harbour. Nobody
  sank. That was always the whole plan."[^s18]
- **Insists on honest accounting even when the story is not flattering** — "I want to be honest
  about the calendar, because this programme has earned its honesty the hard way."[^s19]
- **Finds virtue in the unglamorous, plainly stated** — "That is the platform you are standing on
  now: measured, boring, dependable. In a warehouse system, boring is the highest compliment I
  know."[^s22]
