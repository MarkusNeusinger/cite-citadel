Sabine Krüger is Head of Warehouse Operations at
[Blauwal Logistik GmbH](../organizations/blauwal-logistik-gmbh.md).[^s1] At the
[Projekt LEUCHTFEUER](../projects/projekt-leuchtfeuer.md) kickoff meeting she flagged the state of
[KOMET](../systems/komet.md)'s article master data as the operational risk she loses sleep over:
years of parallel maintenance have left duplicate article records across the sites, and migrating
duplicates would multiply them.[^s2] The meeting agreed without dissent that the master data must
be cleansed before the pilot cutover, not patched afterwards, and gave her ownership of that
cleansing (action AP-1).[^s2][^s3]

She took the minutes for the steering committee (Lenkungsausschuss) meeting on 19 March 2024.[^s4]
At that meeting she reported that the cleansing review is ongoing, that the extent of duplicate
records is significant and has grown over years — especially where sites created articles in
parallel — and that she will now report cleansing metrics to the committee monthly.[^s5]

The programme charter names her the owner of both master data and training for
Projekt LEUCHTFEUER.[^s6]

At the steering committee's extraordinary session of 10 February 2025 she reported the cleansing
at roughly two thirds complete — about two thirds of the duplicate article records identified
across the estate merged or retired, with the remainder concentrated in the two sites with the
oldest parallel maintenance history — and that the trajectory supports the pilot date.[^s7] The
committee kept action AP-1 open with its monthly reporting rhythm and renewed its status as a hard
precondition: no pilot cutover before Walle's slice of the cleansing is finished.[^s7] She was
separately tasked with communicating the 22–23 February 2025 pilot weekend to customers with
Walle-routed traffic (action AP-8, due 14 February 2025).[^s8]

At a status handover on 30 June 2025 she reported the cleansing complete: the duplicate
stragglers still outstanding in May at the two oldest sites are cleansed, and article maintenance
now runs through a central check so duplicates cannot recur. Action AP-1 is closed and drops from
the monthly report, and the group thanked her, noting AP-1 had been the programme's most
persistent point for over fifteen months.[^s9] At the same meeting she presented the pilot's
consolidated operating figures for its first weeks — shipment volume, error rate, and picking
performance all inside plan — and, with [Jörn Albers](jorn-albers.md), reviewed the cutover
weekend for the group from the site's own perspective.[^s10][^s11]

In her 20 March 2026 go-live announcement, marking the full-estate cutover to QUAYSTONE,
Petra Vogelsang credited "Sabine's master-data campaign — fifteen months of unglamorous
persistence that made every cutover after Walle quietly uneventful."[^s12]

She attended the
[SEAGULL (customer portal programme)](../projects/seagull-customer-portal-programme.md) kickoff
meeting on 8 April 2026 as Head of Warehouse Operations; her one condition for the portal, accepted
without debate, is that it shows the operational truth or nothing — a portal that flatters the
numbers would cost more trust than it buys.[^s13] She also owns naming an operations liaison for
the programme from the Bremen-Walle crew, due 20 April 2026 (action SG-AP-3).[^s14]
