Jörn Albers leads [Blauwal Logistik GmbH](../organizations/blauwal-logistik-gmbh.md)'s warehouse
at Bremen-Walle (Lagerleitung Bremen-Walle).[^s1] Walle was chosen as the
[Projekt LEUCHTFEUER](../projects/projekt-leuchtfeuer.md) pilot site partly because his team there
is regarded as the most change-friendly crew in the estate.[^s2]

At the steering committee (Lenkungsausschuss) meeting on 19 March 2024 he pledged his site team's
full support for the pilot, codenamed SEAGULL, but asked that picking (Kommissionierleistung)
capacity be planned realistically during the changeover weeks and that peak loads be kept out of
the pilot.[^s3] He also asked that the new mobile data-capture (MDE) devices for the pilot reach
Walle with enough lead time for his team to practice under real operating conditions before
go-live.[^s4]

At the steering committee's extraordinary session of 10 February 2025 he asked that the pilot
weekend avoid the month-end peak; the chosen 22–23 February 2025 weekend does.[^s5]

The pilot cutover at his site over that weekend was reported a success in Gezeitenwerk's week-one
vendor summary: order release stood still for only four hours over the whole cutover weekend, and
Walle received trucks from 06:00 on Monday, 24 February 2025, as planned.[^s6] Tomás Iglesias
credited the outcome chiefly to his implementation crew, saying "Jörn's team met them more than
halfway; a cutover goes like this only when the site wants it to."[^s7]

> [!CONTRADICTION]
> Gezeitenwerk's week-one report states order release at Walle stood still for only four hours
> over the whole cutover weekend.[^s6] The site's own status handover, four months later, puts the
> operational truth of the weekend at nine hours — order release was fully down from 22:00 on
> Saturday to 07:00 on Sunday; the site had planned for an outage of this length, so no customer
> commitment was broken.[^s8]

At that same 30 June 2025 status handover, he and [Sabine Krüger](sabine-kruger.md) reviewed the
cutover weekend for the group from the site's own perspective: the data takeover was complete with
no unresolved differences, and training together with the MDE practice weeks had left the crew
well prepared.[^s9] He also told the group his crew, sceptical of the
[MDE](../abbreviations/mde-mobile-datenerfassung.md) devices at first, would not give them up now,
and that picking-floor feedback had already driven two improvements to the QUAYSTONE screens the
devices run, which Gezeitenwerk folded into its standard product.[^s10]
