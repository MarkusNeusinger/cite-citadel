Jonas Petersen is the PMO (programme management office) for
[Projekt LEUCHTFEUER](../projects/projekt-leuchtfeuer.md) at
[Blauwal Logistik GmbH](../organizations/blauwal-logistik-gmbh.md).[^s1] He authored the
programme's charter on behalf of the programme lead: version 1.0, issued 14 May 2024, and
Revision B — which supersedes version 1.0 in full — approved by the Lenkungsausschuss by
circular resolution of 17 January 2025.[^s1] Per the charter, his governance role is to keep the
programme's record.[^s2]

He took the minutes at the Projekt LEUCHTFEUER kickoff meeting on 5 March 2024[^s3] and was
present at the Lenkungsausschuss meeting of 19 March 2024.[^s4]

He issued charter Revision B on 20 January 2025, three days after the Lenkungsausschuss's
17 January circular-resolution approval; the committee formally ratified Revision B as decision
D-12 at its extraordinary steering session of 10 February 2025.[^s5] He took the minutes at that
session, held in English at the guest's request, with the German protokoll series resuming the
following session.[^s6]

In Gezeitenwerk's week-one report on the SEAGULL pilot cutover, Tomás Iglesias named him as the
recipient of the full metrics pack for the project share.[^s7]

He was present at the Bremen-Walle pilot's status handover on 30 June 2025.[^s8]

He also took the minutes at the
[SEAGULL (customer portal programme)](../projects/seagull-customer-portal-programme.md) kickoff
meeting on 8 April 2026, chaired by [Yasmin Okafor](yasmin-okafor.md).[^s9]
