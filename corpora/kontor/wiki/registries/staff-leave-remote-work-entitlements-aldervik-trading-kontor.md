This registry captures the annual leave and remote-work-day entitlements defined by staff role in
the Aldervik Trading Kontor staff handbook, which distinguishes warehouse staff, office staff, and
managers.[^s1]

## Roles

- **Warehouse staff** — 25 days of annual leave, 0 remote days per week.[^s2]
- **Office staff** — 28 days of annual leave, 2 remote days per week.[^s3]
- **Managers** — 30 days of annual leave, 3 remote days per week.[^s4]
