# Staff Handbook policies

The Aldervik Trading Kontor Staff Handbook sets out working-time and employment rules for staff.[^s1]

## Customer support hours

Standard customer support hours are Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 CET.[^s2]

## Leave and remote work by role

| Role | Annual leave (days) | Remote days per week |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warehouse staff | 25[^s3] | 0[^s3] |
| Office staff | 28[^s3] | 2[^s3] |
| Managers | 30[^s3] | 3[^s3] |

## Probation

The probation period for new staff is six months.[^s4]
