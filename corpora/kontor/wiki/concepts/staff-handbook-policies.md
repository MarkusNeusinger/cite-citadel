# Staff Handbook Policies

The Aldervik Trading Kontor staff handbook sets out customer support hours, leave and remote-work entitlements by role, and the probation period.[^s1] The company is also documented as [Aldervik Kontor](../organizations/aldervik-kontor.md); the handbook's title names it "Aldervik Trading Kontor".[^s1]

## Customer support hours

Standard customer support hours are Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 CET.[^s2]

## Leave and remote work by role

| Role | Annual leave (days) | Remote days per week |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warehouse staff | 25 | 0 |
| Office staff | 28 | 2 |
| Managers | 30 | 3 |

The table is cited as a whole.[^s3] Warehouse staff get 25 days of annual leave and no remote days.[^s3] Office staff get 28 days of annual leave and 2 remote days per week.[^s3] Managers get 30 days of annual leave and 3 remote days per week.[^s3]

## Probation

The probation period for new staff is six months.[^s4]
