# Warehouse Manager onboarding checklist

[Aldervik Kontor](../organizations/aldervik-kontor.md) has an onboarding checklist for new Warehouse Managers.[^s1] It names three steps.

- **Safety induction:** complete the warehouse safety induction within the first 10 working days.[^s2]
- **Tidewater Clause:** sign the Tidewater Clause during the first week.[^s3] HR holds the full terms on file.[^s3] The checklist does not state what the clause covers.[^s3]
- **Shadowing:** shadow an experienced shift lead at the Hamburg warehouse for two shifts.[^s4]
