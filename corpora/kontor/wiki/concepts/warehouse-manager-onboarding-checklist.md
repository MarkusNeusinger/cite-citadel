# Warehouse Manager Onboarding Checklist

[Aldervik Kontor](../organizations/aldervik-kontor.md) publishes a checklist titled "Warehouse Manager Onboarding Checklist".[^s1] It lists three steps for a new warehouse manager.

- **Safety induction:** the warehouse safety induction is to be completed within the first 10 working days.[^s2]
- **Tidewater Clause:** the new manager signs the Tidewater Clause during the first week. HR holds the full terms on file.[^s3] The checklist does not say what the clause covers.
- **Shadowing:** the new manager shadows an experienced shift lead at the Hamburg warehouse for two shifts.[^s4]
