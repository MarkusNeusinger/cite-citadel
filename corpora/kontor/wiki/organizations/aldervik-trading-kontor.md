Aldervik Trading Kontor was founded in 2011 in Aldervik harbour.[^s1] Its original business was
importing dried goods and textiles.[^s1]

As of early 2019, the company operated two warehouses, in Rotterdam and Hamburg, and the team was
about 40 people.[^s3] By year-end 2019, the company had 38 staff.[^s2] It reported revenue of
EUR 4.1 million for 2019.[^s2]

Standard customer support hours are Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 CET.[^s4] The probation
period for new staff is six months.[^s5]

By Q3 2026, revenue had grown to EUR 6.4 million, up from EUR 5.8 million in Q2 2026.[^s6] A
chart included in the same review reports gross margin at 34.2%.[^s10] The company employed 142
staff across four departments in Q3 2026[^s7] — against the 138 FTE the 2026 budget allocated
across departments[^s8]. Warehouses by then operated in Rotterdam, Hamburg, and Gdansk.[^s9]

A 2026 sustainability report summary states that warehouse energy use fell 12 percent year over
year.[^s12]
