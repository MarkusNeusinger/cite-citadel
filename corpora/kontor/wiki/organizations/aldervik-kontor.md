# Aldervik Kontor

Aldervik Kontor was founded in 2011 in Aldervik harbour.[^s1] Its original business was importing dried goods and textiles.[^s1]

The company's 2018 kickoff deck is the source of these founding facts.[^s1]

## Early 2019

An internal memo dated 12 March 2019 states that, as of early 2019, Aldervik operated two warehouses, in Rotterdam and Hamburg.[^s3] It puts the team at about 40 people.[^s3]

## 2019 year-end figures

The company's 2019 year-end summary records a staff of 38.[^s2] It records revenue of EUR 4.1 million for 2019.[^s2]

## Q3 2026 business review

The Q3 2026 business review deck reports revenue of EUR 6.4 million for Q3 2026, up from EUR 5.8 million in Q2 2026.[^s4][^s5] It states that the company employs 142 staff across four departments.[^s6] It lists warehouses in Rotterdam, Hamburg, and Gdansk.[^s7] Earlier sources named only Rotterdam and Hamburg for early 2019,[^s3] so Gdansk is a later addition.

A chart image embedded in the deck is headed "GROSS MARGIN 34.2%", which gives a gross margin of 34.2% (the figure is partly obscured by image noise).[^s8]

The speaker notes say the board has not approved a Lisbon warehouse and call its planned Q4 2026 opening tentative.[^s9]

## 2026 sustainability report

A summary document titled "Aldervik 2026 sustainability report (summary)" reports that warehouse energy use fell 12 percent year over year.[^s10][^s11]

## Open Points

- Whether the Lisbon warehouse, planned to open in Q4 2026, will be approved by the board: as of the Q3 2026 review the speaker notes say it is not yet approved (2026-10-22).[^s9]
