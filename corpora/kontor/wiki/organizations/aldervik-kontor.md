# Aldervik Kontor

Aldervik Kontor is the company presented in a 2018 kickoff deck.[^s1] It was founded in 2011 in Aldervik harbour.[^s1] Its original business was importing dried goods and textiles.[^s1]

## 2019 year-end figures

A 2019 year-end summary for "Aldervik" reports a staff of 38 and revenue of EUR 4.1 million.[^s2]

## Early 2019 operations

An internal memo dated 12 March 2019 states that, as of early 2019, Aldervik operated two warehouses, in Rotterdam and Hamburg.[^s3] The same memo puts the team at about 40 people.[^s3] The 2019 year-end summary reports a staff of 38,[^s2] a later and exact figure against the memo's early-2019 approximation.

## Q3 2026 business review

A Q3 2026 business review deck reports revenue of EUR 6.4 million for Q3 2026, up from EUR 5.8 million in Q2 2026.[^s5]

The deck states that the company employs 142 staff across four departments.[^s6] The [Budget 2026](../concepts/budget-2026.md) workbook plans 138 full-time equivalents (FTE) for 2026;[^s7] the two figures are not like-for-like (a headcount of staff against planned FTE), but the review's 142 is 4 above the plan's 138.

Per the deck, warehouses operate in Rotterdam, Hamburg, and Gdansk.[^s8] This extends the two warehouses (Rotterdam and Hamburg) the March 2019 memo reports for early 2019.[^s3]

An embedded chart in the deck is titled "GROSS MARGIN 34.2%"; the extract does not say which period it covers, and the chart's bars carry no labels.[^s9]

### Lisbon warehouse (tentative)

The review's speaker notes state that the board has not approved a Lisbon warehouse, and that the planned Q4 2026 opening is to be treated as tentative.[^s10]

## 2026 sustainability report

A summary of the Aldervik 2026 sustainability report states that warehouse energy use fell 12 percent year over year.[^s11]

## Onboarding

New Warehouse Managers follow an onboarding checklist that includes shadowing a shift lead at the Hamburg warehouse; see [Warehouse Manager onboarding checklist](../concepts/warehouse-manager-onboarding-checklist.md).[^s4]
