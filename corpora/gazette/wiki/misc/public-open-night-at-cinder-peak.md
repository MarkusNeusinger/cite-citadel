# Public Open Night at Cinder Peak

[Cinder Peak Observatory](../organizations/cinder-peak-observatory.md), through its outreach desk, invites the public to an open night on Saturday 13 June 2026.[^s1]

## Schedule

- Doors open at 21:30 local time (BST) on the visitor terrace, and the evening ends at 00:30.[^s1]
- Visitors tour the dome and look through the new 1.2-metre telescope, weather permitting.[^s1]

## Booking

- Entry is free, but places are limited and must be booked in advance with the observatory's outreach desk (outreach@cinderpeak.example).[^s1]

## Practical notes

- Visitors are asked to bring warm clothing, because the terrace is unheated and exposed.[^s2]
- If the sky is overcast, the dome tour goes ahead and the telescope session is replaced by a talk in the visitor room.[^s2]
