This registry captures the second-season readings for all thirty-six fixed stations (T-01
through T-36) of the [Cinder Peak Treeline Transect](../projects/cinder-peak-treeline-transect.md),
walked by [The Meridian Gazette](../organizations/the-meridian-gazette.md)'s environment
desk.[^s1] Each station is revisited twice a season — at the opening of the melt and again
at its close — and reported in the same fixed shape: position, stand age from the oldest
cored stem, stem density over the fixed plot, canopy closure, and any damage found; a
station with no reading is reported as having none.[^s1] See the transect's project page for
the survey method and this season's overall pattern.
