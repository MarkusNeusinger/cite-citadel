# Sensor calibration offsets — Cinder Peak Observatory

This registry lists the six sensors and their calibration offsets from the Sensor Calibration Sheet issued by the instrument desk of [Cinder Peak Observatory](../organizations/cinder-peak-observatory.md) on 22 April 2026 and checked by [Ines Varga](../persons/ines-varga.md).[^s1] The sheet gives the offsets without a unit.[^s1]

- **Dome Humidity Probe** — offset 0.12.[^s1]
- **Mirror Cell Thermistor** — offset 0.35.[^s1]
- **Primary Focus Encoder** — offset 0.08.[^s1]
- **Seeing Monitor Camera** — offset 0.21.[^s1]
- **Wind Mast Anemometer** — offset 0.47.[^s1]
- **Sky Quality Meter** — offset 0.16.[^s1]

## How the offsets are used

Each offset is subtracted from the sensor's raw reading before the value enters the nightly log.[^s2] The six sensors were checked against the instrument desk's reference set on 21 April 2026, and the sheet replaces every earlier calibration sheet for these sensors.[^s2] The next scheduled calibration is due in October 2026, or sooner if any sensor is moved or replaced.[^s2] Questions about the sheet go to the instrument desk.[^s2]
