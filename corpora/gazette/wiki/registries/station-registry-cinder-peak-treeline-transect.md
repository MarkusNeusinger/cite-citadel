# Station registry — Cinder Peak treeline transect

This registry lists every station of the [Cinder Peak treeline transect](../projects/cinder-peak-treeline-transect.md): a fixed line of thirty-six plots followed by the Meridian Gazette environment desk, as reported in the second-season report.[^s37] Each station is reported in the same shape — position, stand age (ring count of the oldest cored stem), stem density over the fixed ten-by-ten-metre plot (living standing stems only; deadwood is tallied separately), canopy closure (densiometer, mean of four cardinal readings from the plot centre) and damage.[^s38] Every station was walked once in the first week of the season and once at its close.[^s38] The report states that nothing in it is interpolated and that a station with no reading is reported as having none; "none recorded" below is the report's own wording for damage.[^s37]

Format per entry: elevation, aspect, substrate; stand age (ring count); stem density; canopy closure; damage.
