# Station registry — Cinder Peak treeline transect

This registry lists the thirty-six plots of the [Cinder Peak treeline transect](../concepts/cinder-peak-treeline-transect.md) as reported in its second-season report by the Meridian Gazette environment desk.[^s37] Each station is reported in the same order and shape: position, stand age from the oldest cored stem, stem density, canopy closure and damage found.[^s37] Stand age is the ring count of the oldest cored stem.[^s1] Stem density is live standing stems over a fixed ten-by-ten-metre plot, excluding separately tallied deadwood.[^s1] Canopy closure is a densiometer percentage averaged over four cardinal readings from the plot centre.[^s1] Elevations are in metres.[^s1]
