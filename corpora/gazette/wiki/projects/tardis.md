TARDIS was a 2007 experiment that carried [tardigrades](../objects/tardigrade.md) aboard the
European FOTON-M3 satellite into low Earth orbit and exposed them directly to the vacuum of
space and the Sun's unfiltered ultraviolet radiation.[^s1]

Some tardigrades survived the vacuum, and a number of those also survived the radiation and
went on to reproduce after returning to Earth.[^s1]
