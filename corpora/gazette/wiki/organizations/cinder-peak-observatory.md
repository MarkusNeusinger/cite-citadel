# Cinder Peak Observatory

Cinder Peak Observatory has an instrument desk that issued the Sensor Calibration Sheet of 22 April 2026.[^s1] The desk holds a reference set against which the observatory's sensors were checked on 21 April 2026, and it is the contact for questions about the sheet.[^s2] The offsets are listed in the [sensor calibration registry](../registries/sensor-calibration-offsets-cinder-peak-observatory.md).

The observatory opened in 1998.[^s3] Its seeing measurements are covered in [Atmospheric seeing at Cinder Peak Observatory](../concepts/atmospheric-seeing-at-cinder-peak-observatory.md); its new instrument offers about three times the light-gathering area of the retired 0.6-metre telescope.[^s4]

Its new [1.2-metre telescope](../objects/cinder-peak-1-2-metre-telescope.md) reached first light on 1 May 2026;[^s6] the press release says it roughly quadruples the light-gathering area, which differs from the "about three times" above (see the telescope page).[^s6]

Its outreach desk invited the public to the [Public Open Night at Cinder Peak](../misc/public-open-night-at-cinder-peak.md) on 13 June 2026.[^s5]

## Dome resurfacing closure, April 2026

A public notice from the observatory states that public viewing nights are suspended for dome resurfacing from 3 to 17 April 2026.[^s7] The notice says the observatory reopens to visitors on 18 April 2026, and refers readers to the Meridian Gazette for updates.[^s8]
