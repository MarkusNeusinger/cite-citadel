The Meridian Gazette publishes a Science section, which ran the article "How Water Bears
Survive the Void" about [tardigrades](../objects/tardigrade.md).[^s1] The Gazette's science
desk continues coverage of the topic beyond this article.[^s2]

The Gazette also has an environment desk, which maintains the [Cinder Peak Treeline
Transect](../projects/cinder-peak-treeline-transect.md), a fixed 36-plot monitoring line it
walks twice each season.[^s3]

[Cinder Peak Observatory](../organizations/cinder-peak-observatory.md) names the Gazette as its
newspaper of record.[^s4]
