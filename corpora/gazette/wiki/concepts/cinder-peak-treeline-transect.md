# Cinder Peak treeline transect

The Cinder Peak treeline transect is a fixed line of thirty-six plots that the Meridian Gazette environment desk has followed since the transect was established.[^s1] Every station is revisited twice a season, at the opening of the melt and again at its close.[^s1] The report on the second full walk states that nothing in it is interpolated: a station with no reading is reported as having none.[^s1]

## Method

Each plot is a fixed ten-by-ten-metre square.[^s2] Stand age is taken from the ring count of the oldest cored stem at the station.[^s2] Stem density counts live, standing stems only; deadwood is tallied separately.[^s2] Canopy closure is measured by densiometer, averaged over four cardinal readings from the plot centre.[^s2] Each plot is photographed from its north-west corner on both visits, and the photographs are archived with the survey rather than reproduced in the report.[^s2]

Per-station readings are in the [station registry](../registries/station-registry-cinder-peak-treeline-transect.md). The stations run from T-01 (1197 m) to T-36 (1792 m).[^s3][^s4]

## Second-season finding

The desk reports that across the full transect canopy closure fell with elevation while stand age did not, and reads this as recruitment failing at the upper stations rather than the older stands there dying back.[^s5] This is the desk's interpretation. The transect will be walked again next season on the same schedule.[^s5]
