Astronomical seeing is the blurring of stars caused by atmospheric turbulence.[^s1] It is reported in arcseconds, where a lower value indicates a sharper (less blurred) image.[^s1]

[Cinder Peak Observatory](../organizations/cinder-peak-observatory.md) tracks seeing as a median nightly value and reports monthly medians in its annual brief.[^s2] For 2025, the autumn nights were consistently the sharpest of the year.[^s2] The brief's chart shows the shortest (sharpest) monthly bars in September, October, and November, with November the shortest of all, and annotates the single best night measured in 2025 — 0.42 arcsec, recorded November 14; the tallest (poorest-seeing) bars fall in June and July.[^s3]
