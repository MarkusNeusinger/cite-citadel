# Atmospheric seeing at Cinder Peak Observatory

Seeing is the blurring of stars by atmospheric turbulence; it is reported in arcseconds, and lower values are sharper.[^s1] The Atmospheric Seeing Brief - 2025 summarises the median nightly seeing measured at [Cinder Peak Observatory](../organizations/cinder-peak-observatory.md), which opened in 1998, through 2025.[^s1]

## 2025 findings

- The brief states that the autumn nights were consistently the sharpest of the year.[^s1]
- Figure 1 plots the median nightly seeing for each month of 2025 as a bar chart (axis in arcseconds, with no numeric scale on the vertical axis). November has the lowest bar, with September and October the next lowest.[^s2]
- Figure 1 annotates the best value as 0.42 arcseconds on 14 November.[^s2]
- The brief reports medians only; raw nightly logs are archived internally and are not reproduced in it.[^s3]

## Instrument

The survey will continue with the observatory's new instrument, which offers about three times the light-gathering area of the retired 0.6-metre telescope.[^s3] The press release says the [1.2-metre telescope](../objects/cinder-peak-1-2-metre-telescope.md) roughly quadruples the light-gathering area (see the contradiction noted there) and will be used mainly for variable-star monitoring and this seeing survey.[^s4]
