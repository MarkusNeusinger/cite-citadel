Cryptobiosis is a dormant state that lets an organism endure near-total desiccation.[^s1] When
the loss of water specifically drives the dormant state, biologists call it anhydrobiosis.[^s1]

[Tardigrades](../objects/tardigrade.md) enter cryptobiosis by drying out, losing roughly 97
percent of their body water in the process.[^s2]
