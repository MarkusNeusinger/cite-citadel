# TDP — Tardigrade-specific Intrinsically Disordered Proteins

TDPs are tardigrade-specific intrinsically disordered proteins that a drying tardigrade floods its cells with; they vitrify (turn glassy) as the animal dries, cradling proteins and membranes so they do not shatter.[^s1]
