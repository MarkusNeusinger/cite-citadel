[Tardigrades](../objects/tardigrade.md) flood their cells with tardigrade-specific
intrinsically disordered proteins (TDPs) as they dry.[^s1] TDPs vitrify — turn glassy — during
drying, cradling proteins and membranes so they do not shatter.[^s1]
