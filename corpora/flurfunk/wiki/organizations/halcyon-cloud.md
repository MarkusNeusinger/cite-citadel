According to [Dana Kessler](../persons/dana-kessler.md)'s cover letter, she has spent the last
four years (as of her March 2026 job application) as a Staff Engineer at Halcyon Cloud, owning its
event ingestion path.[^s1] She states she cut end-to-end processing latency there by more than
half and led the migration of its streaming layer onto Kafka.[^s1] Her CV lists this role's dates
as 2022–2026, its location as Amsterdam, and adds that she mentored two engineers through the
Kafka migration.[^s2]
