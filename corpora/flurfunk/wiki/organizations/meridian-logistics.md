According to [Dana Kessler](../persons/dana-kessler.md)'s CV, she worked as a Backend Engineer at
Meridian Logistics in Rotterdam from 2015 to 2018, before joining
[Cobalt Systems](../organizations/cobalt-systems.md).[^s1] She built and maintained internal APIs
for the logistics tracking system, and was the first engineer to introduce automated integration
testing to the backend team.[^s1]
