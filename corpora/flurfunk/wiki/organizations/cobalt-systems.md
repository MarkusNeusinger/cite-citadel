According to [Dana Kessler](../persons/dana-kessler.md)'s cover letter, she spent four years at
Cobalt Systems before joining [Halcyon Cloud](../organizations/halcyon-cloud.md).[^s1] She states
she rebuilt a batch pipeline into a streaming one there, and that "a good chunk of the company"
still runs on it today.[^s1] Her CV lists this role as Senior Software Engineer at Cobalt Systems,
Berlin, from 2018 to 2022, and adds that she introduced backpressure handling that eliminated a
recurring class of overnight outages, and was on-call lead for the data platform for two
years.[^s2]
