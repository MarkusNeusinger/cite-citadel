# Sofia Ruiz

- Sofia Ruiz posts on the Larkspur Community Forum as "Sofia Ruiz (Larkspur Support)", i.e. as a support representative of [Larkspur](../organizations/larkspur.md).[^s1]
- On 2026-02-19 she posted the accepted answer to a thread about Skylight dashboards frozen after a timezone change, naming the [janitor](../systems/janitor.md) service's cached UTC offset as the cause and giving the `SKYLIGHT_TZ` fix.[^s1]
- She offered to dig in further if the problem persisted after the restart.[^s2]

## In the #platform channel

- 2026-02-10: relayed [Northwind](../organizations/northwind.md)'s ticket about empty dashboards for older ranges and, on 2026-02-11, the loss of three weeks of audit history under the 7-day retention window.[^s3]
- 2026-02-11: proposed raising the default event-retention window to 30 days, recorded the decision, owned the PR and changelog note, and merged it; see [Skylight default event-retention window](../concepts/skylight-event-retention-default.md).[^s4]
- 2026-02-10: said she would work from Larkspur's Berlin office on Thursday and Friday.[^s5]
- 2026-02-11: offered to take the `retention-svc` → `janitor` rename; see [janitor](../systems/janitor.md).[^s6]
