## Style profile

Observations from one source, a cover letter written in English.

- **Register:** warm, conversational and candid for a formal genre; opens with a confessional aside. Example: "I'll be honest — I don't usually get excited about job postings, but this one made me stop scrolling."[^s2]
- **Idiom:** informal, plumbing-themed metaphors for infrastructure work. Examples: "the unglamorous plumbing that keeps data systems honest" and "the queues nobody thinks about until they break".[^s8]
- **Self-description:** brief and understated, framed as a personal preference. Example: "I like being the person who makes the fast path fast and the slow path disappear."[^s7]
- **Rhythm and devices:** first person throughout, em dashes, contractions, and short closing lines. Examples: "Thanks for reading this far." and sign-off "Warmly,".[^s9]
- **Vague quantifiers over exact figures:** "a good chunk of the company", "more than half", "a decade or so".[^s7]
- **CV register:** terse and impersonal, in short past-tense bullets without pronouns, with more concrete figures than the letter. Examples: "Cut end-to-end event processing latency by more than 50%." and "On-call lead for the data platform for two years."[^s12][^s13]
