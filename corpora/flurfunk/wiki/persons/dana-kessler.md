Dana Kessler is a platform engineer based in Amsterdam, Netherlands.[^s1][^s9] Her CV lists her
current professional identity as "Senior / Staff Platform Engineer."[^s9]

## Career

Kessler states she has spent roughly the last decade building "the unglamorous plumbing that
keeps data systems honest: ingestion pipelines, storage layers, the queues nobody thinks about
until they break."[^s4]

Her CV states she has about 11 years of experience building data ingestion, storage, and
streaming infrastructure, and that she is comfortable "owning a system end to end — from the
queue to the query."[^s10]

At [Halcyon Cloud](../organizations/halcyon-cloud.md), Kessler has spent the last four years (as
of her March 2026 application) as a Staff Engineer owning the event ingestion path; she states she
cut end-to-end processing latency there by more than half and led the migration of the streaming
layer onto Kafka.[^s5] Her CV lists this role's dates as 2022–2026, its location as Amsterdam, and
adds that she mentored two engineers through the Kafka migration.[^s11]

Before Halcyon Cloud, Kessler spent four years at [Cobalt Systems](../organizations/cobalt-systems.md),
where she says she rebuilt a batch pipeline into a streaming one that "a good chunk of the
company" still runs on today.[^s5] Her CV lists this role as Senior Software Engineer at Cobalt
Systems, Berlin, from 2018 to 2022, and adds that she introduced backpressure handling that
eliminated a recurring class of overnight outages, and was on-call lead for the data platform for
two years.[^s12]

Kessler sums this up as "the better part of eleven years in this kind of plumbing," and describes
herself as liking "being the person who makes the fast path fast and the slow path disappear."[^s5]

Before Cobalt Systems, her CV lists a Backend Engineer role at
[Meridian Logistics](../organizations/meridian-logistics.md) in Rotterdam from 2015 to 2018, where
she built and maintained internal APIs for the logistics tracking system and was the first
engineer to introduce automated integration testing to the backend team.[^s13]

## Education

Her CV states she holds a BSc in Computer Science from TU Delft (2015).[^s14]

## Skills

Her CV lists her skills as Python, Go, Kafka, and Kubernetes.[^s15]

## Application to Larkspur

In March 2026, Kessler applied for the Senior Platform Engineer role at
[Larkspur](../organizations/larkspur.md).[^s2][^s3] She wrote that she does not usually get
excited about job postings, but that this one made her "stop scrolling."[^s3]

She said what draws her to Larkspur is that the company seems to care about the same
"boring-but-critical" infrastructure problems she does, with what she called a much sharper
product on top.[^s4] She said she has been following [Skylight](../systems/skylight.md) and that
its "recent sub-second refresh work genuinely impressed" her; in her words, getting a live
analytics dashboard to refresh in under a second without melting the backend "is not a marketing
line to me — I know exactly how hard that is."[^s4]

Kessler said she wants to do that kind of work somewhere small enough that it matters, on a
product she would actually use, and that she considers Larkspur to fit on both counts.[^s6] Her CV
similarly states she is looking for a senior platform role at a small, product-focused
team.[^s10]

Her cover letter states that her CV was attached separately with her full timeline and
details.[^s7]
