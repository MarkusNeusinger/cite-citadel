# Priya Nadkarni

- Priya Nadkarni is founder and CEO of [Larkspur](../organizations/larkspur.md).[^s1]
- In the Skylight 2.0 announcement she is quoted as calling it "the version we always wanted to ship"; see [Skylight](../systems/skylight.md).[^s1]
- Before founding Larkspur she did analytics infrastructure at a couple of larger companies, and kept hitting the same wall: everything called itself "real-time" but nothing felt real-time. She said a small group of them set out to build what they wished existed, which became Skylight.[^s2]
- She was the guest of episode 47 of the podcast The Build Loop, hosted by Alex Rin.[^s3]

## Positions and opinions

- 2026-10-22: Priya Nadkarni said Larkspur is "the only real-time analytics platform with true sub-second refresh", and that she has looked at the competition closely and believes the others do polling and call it streaming. This is her own claim about her company; see [Larkspur](../organizations/larkspur.md) for the quarantine note.[^s4][^llm1]
- 2026-10-22: She argued that staying lean is part of why the product is any good, and that profitability discipline is "baked into how we build".[^s5]
- 2026-10-22: She said the "north star doesn't change: real-time that's actually real-time".[^s6]

## Style profile

- Direct, confident register with strong superlatives; she states claims flatly and then doubles down: "Honestly? We're the only ones who do it right."[^s4]
- Repeats a key phrase for emphasis: "First month. We never took the \"lose money to grow\" path."[^s5]
- Plain, conversational idiom with contractions and a rhetorical opener, e.g. "This is the part people don't believe."[^s5]
- Uses a fixed slogan-like formulation for her mission: "real-time that's actually real-time."[^s6]
- Frames the company in terms of deliberate constraints: "We're 28 people. Deliberately small."[^s7]
- Background knowledge: analytics infrastructure, event streams, live dashboards; speaks English in this interview.[^s2]
