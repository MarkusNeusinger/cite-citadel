# Platform team retros

The quarterly retro summaries for the platform team were reconstructed afterwards by a contractor from the chat history.[^s1] The platform team has not confirmed them.[^s1]

The summaries are written in the team's voice ("we"), but this is how the contractor drafted them; nobody on the team wrote or signed off on them.[^s2] The folder's note says every statement in the retro files is to be treated as a reconstruction until the team confirms it.[^s2]

Facts taken from the retro files should therefore be attributed as the contractor's reconstruction, not as the team's own statements.
