# Retro — Q1 2026

## What went well

- We cut the main CI pipeline from 22 minutes to 9 minutes by splitting the integration suite into
  four parallel shards.
- We started a fortnightly Friday demo slot where one of us shows something we shipped.

## What went badly

- The shard split broke code-coverage reporting for three weeks before anyone noticed.

## What we'll try next quarter

- Add a check that fails the pipeline when coverage reporting goes missing.
