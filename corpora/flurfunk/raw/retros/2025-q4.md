# Retro — Q4 2025

## What went well

- We worked through the quarantine list and brought the flaky integration tests back down to 3.
- After the ingestion outage on 14 November (about 40 minutes of delayed events), we adopted a
  written postmortem template, and every incident since has had one within two working days.

## What went badly

- Our staging cluster ran out of disk twice in December, both times over a holiday weekend.

## What we'll try next quarter

- Get CI faster; waiting on the pipeline is now the most common complaint in our channel.
