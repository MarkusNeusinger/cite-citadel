# Retro — Q3 2025

## What went well

- We moved the on-call rotation from fortnightly to weekly shifts, with the handoff every Monday at
  10:00. Nobody carried the pager for more than seven days in a row this quarter.
- We wrote our first shared runbook for the ingestion workers and kept it in the platform repo.

## What went badly

- The integration test suite was flaky for most of August. We quarantined 14 tests and still lost
  roughly two weeks of merge throughput to re-runs.

## What we'll try next quarter

- Give the flaky-test quarantine a hard owner and a weekly review slot.
